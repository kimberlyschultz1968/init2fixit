"Were you here Tuesday?"

The single most common call a landscape office gets. Right now it costs a radio call, a crew leader's memory, and a callback. Here it's a timestamp and photos.

Hours over budget is where margin dies

The contract is a fixed price. Every hour a crew spends over the budgeted time on a property comes straight out of profit — and on paper route sheets nobody ever adds it up.

Skipped stops stop disappearing

Rained out, gate locked, dog in the yard. If nobody logs it, it never gets made up — and the property manager is the one who notices.
